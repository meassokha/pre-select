# Design brief
style: clay (Soft 3D, adapted for banking; same as the NBO prototype) | theme: both (toggle) | purpose: finance / banking back-office | motion: expressive
accent: Wing Bank green #98C20A (CTAs) + blue #0077FF (sparing) | font: Inter + Noto Sans Khmer | stack: react-vite-tailwind | sidebar default: expanded
Rules: puffy shadows on cards/buttons/icon chips only; table rows, charts and numbers stay flat; semantic colours for status (green ok, red knock-out/risk, amber warning).
